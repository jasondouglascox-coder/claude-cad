# Grover & Pals keychain — design spec

Brand source: groverandpals.com logo (gold ring badge with brown paw prints, orange tabby
"Grover" peeking over the rim, gold ribbon banner "GROVER & PALS" in dark brown).

## Agreed
- Front: logo badge (option A), ~50 mm wide (banner is the widest part).
- 4 filaments: gold, orange, white, dark brown (#3E2F27). Bambu A1 + AMS lite, PETG/PLA.
- Raised-layer relief: disk < ring < cat < banner. Fine brown details (outlines, paws,
  eyes, text) are inlaid flush in the top 0.4 mm of the region they sit on.
- Back: QR code to https://groverandpals.com (version 2, ECC M, 25x25), brown modules inlaid
  flush in a white panel. Prints face-down on the bed, so the QR is the smooth first layers.
  Back banner reads "groverandpals.com".
- Keyring tab with a through hole at the top.

## Rules
- All dimensions in `grover/params.py`; Z levels are multiples of 0.2 mm layer height.
- No supports: every color body sits on the bed or on material below it.
- Color bodies never overlap; together they form one connected solid.
- QR on the rendered back must decode to the URL (OpenCV) — the real acceptance test.
- Outputs: colored STEP (Onshape), one STL per color + a multi-part 3MF (Bambu Studio),
  front/back PNG previews.
