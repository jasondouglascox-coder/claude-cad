# Grover & Pals keychain — design spec

## Source
`art/logo.svg` (brand print logo: 3544 px raster + vector URL text). The raster is traced;
the vector URL text is not used on the keychain (the QR on the back carries the URL).

## Agreed
- Full logo silhouette, 50 mm wide (ribbon tip to tip), ~39 mm tall, 4.4 mm thick.
- 4 filaments: Grover orange, yellow, white, dark brown. Art colors map:
  gold ring -> orange, lemon ribbon + folds -> yellow, cream/beige/tan -> white,
  pink nose/tongue -> brown, cat oranges -> orange.
- Raised layers by zone: disk 2.8 < ring 3.2 < ribbon tails 3.4 < cat 3.6 < ribbon 4.0 < paws 4.4.
- The art's cat outline (~0.16 mm at this size) is too thin to print, so every relief step gets a
  generated 0.55 mm brown outline on its raised side. Whiskers / fur strokes are dropped.
  Solid brown art (eyes, paw prints, letters) is kept, shrunk 0.1 mm to open toe/letter gaps.
- Keyring: 3 mm hole through the ring at 12 o'clock, replacing the top paw print, ringed in
  brown, 2.0 mm of plastic to the outer edge. No protruding tab.
- Back: QR to https://groverandpals.com (v2-M, 0.85 mm modules), brown modules inlaid in a white
  panel, printed face-down so the QR is the smooth first layers.

## Pipeline
trace.py (classify + zone + cleanup rasters) -> vector.py (smooth polygons, priority partition,
prisms) -> model.py (one fused body per filament) -> build.py (STEP, STL x4, 3MF, previews).

## Acceptance (tests/test_keychain.py)
Valid bodies, flat on bed, whole-layer Z levels, colors never overlap, one connected piece,
no overhangs, hole size/wall/through-all, no color patch thinner than 0.3 mm from the top,
QR decodes from the rendered back, QR panel inside the badge, 3MF round-trips 4 objects.
