# Grover & Pals keychain v2 — design spec

Supersedes `2026-10-12-keychain.md` (v1, kept in `archive/v1/`, git tag `v1-sketch`). v1's Grover was redrawn by
hand and read as a corgi; v2 takes the cat from the logo's own line work.

## Goal
A 50 mm wide, four-color (orange, yellow, white, brown) keychain that looks like `art/logo.svg`, printed on a
Bambu A1 with AMS lite at 0.16 mm layers, with as few filament swaps as the relief allows.

## Source art
`art/logo.svg` embeds a 3544 px raster of the logo (the only vector content is URL text, unused). At 50 mm the
raster is 61.6 px/mm. The logo's palette maps to filaments:
gold ring, cat oranges, ear interiors -> orange; lemon ribbon and its folds -> yellow; cat whites, tans, cream
backdrop -> white; dark ink, pink nose, eye iris/pupil -> brown. Pink tongue, white fang, whiskers and ear tufts
are dropped (too fine for a 0.4 mm nozzle).

## Art pipeline (grover/art.py, ink.py, regions.py)
1. Classify every raster pixel to the nearest palette color (Lab distance), smooth away anti-aliasing.
2. **Ink**: the dark class is split into thick marks (survive a morphological opening at `ink_thick`: ring outline,
   letters, paw prints, nose, eye rims) kept as traced shapes, and thin strokes reduced to their skeleton and
   re-stroked at `line_w` (0.5 mm). Strokes thinner than `ink_min_src` in the art (whiskers) are dropped first.
   Every brown mark on the print is at least `line_w` wide.
3. **Fills** per filament from the classified map, cleaned with openings of `fill_min` so nothing thinner than a
   nozzle width survives, then vectorized (blur, threshold, contour, Douglas-Peucker at `vec_tol`).
4. **Construction**: the ring (outer radius R) and backdrop disc (radius r) are fitted circles; their brown circles
   are drawn, not traced. Eyes are fitted circles with one circular white glint each. The figure (cat + ribbon,
   with their ink) is translated down by `figure_drop` so the ear tips clear the ring's outer circle by `ear_clear`
   and less orange shows under the ribbon; the ribbon's folds, which end at the ring in the logo, are swept down
   so they still meet it. The ring's paw prints are copies of the logo's 12 o'clock print, stamped every
   `paw_print_step` degrees on the circle midway between the rims, pointing outward, pads backed off to `pad_gap`;
   a print the ribbon would cut is left out, and the 12 o'clock position is the keyring hole with a brown ring.
   Whiskers (thin strokes beside the nose that are not the head's outline or a paw's edge) and the tongue are
   dropped; stroke ends are trimmed of skeleton spurs; the ribbon letters are the art's exact marks, smoothed.
5. Priority (front, high to low): top-band features > face-band ink > cat > ribbon > ring circles/paw prints >
   ring > disc. Lower regions are clipped by higher ones.

## Print stack (print orientation: back on the plate, front up)
Layers: 0.2 mm (`Printer(layer=0.2)`); every Z level is a layer boundary.

| Band | Layers | Z (mm) | Content | Colors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Back inlay | 1–3 | 0 – 0.6 | white QR panel (modules are the brown body) + white URL text, mirrored | brown, white |
| Body | 4–9 | 0.6 – 1.8 | solid brown | brown |
| Base | 10–12 | 1.8 – 2.4 | white under disc, cat and ribbon; orange ring elsewhere | white, orange |
| Face | 13–14 | 2.4 – 2.8 | yellow ribbon, white cat + orange patches, brown ink of cat and ribbon; ring circles, paw prints and hole ring rise 1 layer (to 2.6) | all four |
| Top | 15–16 | 2.8 – 3.2 | white paws and eye glints, brown eyes, nose and ribbon letters | white, brown |

Swaps (colors − 1 per layer): back 3, base 3, face 6, top 2 = about 14 (v1: about 39). The brown ink is raised
above the fills rather than inlaid beside them, which keeps the base band at two colors; the white base under the
ribbon and cat keeps yellow and white bright over the dark body. Nothing starts over air: every band rests on
the band below (checked with `cadkit.checks.unsupported`).

## Dimensions
Width 50 mm ribbon tip to tip (logo scale), ring Ø ≈ 37.1, disc Ø ≈ 25.5, thickness 3.2. Keyring hole Ø 3.5
at 12 o'clock, 2.0 mm wall to the outer edge.

## Back
QR: `HTTPS://GROVERANDPALS.COM` (alphanumeric mode, version 1, 21 modules, error level L), modules `qr_module`
= 0.9 mm (2.25 nozzle widths; survives the ~0.1 mm first-layer squish; 1.0 left no room for the URL), quiet zone 2 modules, white panel with
rounded corners centered in the ring circle below the hole. URL `groverandpals.com` in white, Avenir Next Bold
3.6 mm set glyph by glyph with 0.45 mm tracking (strokes and the brown between glyphs survive a 0.4 mm nozzle),
across the ribbon band under the panel. Both mirrored so they read from the back. The back is the first
layer on the plate, the crispest surface the printer makes; a textured plate gives the matte finish scanners
prefer.

## Acceptance (tests/)
Each filament body valid and watertight; the fused keychain is one solid, flat on the bed, inside the bed; every
horizontal face on a layer boundary; colors never overlap; each band rests on the one below; colors per layer
match the table; brown marks ≥ line_w wide; inlays ≥ `min_island`; ear tips clear the ring circle; hole size,
wall and chamfer; QR decodes from a render of the back, also with the modules grown by the first-layer squish;
URL text mirrored and inside the silhouette; 3MF opens with a filament per body and (slow) slices in Bambu Studio
with the expected color bands.

## Amendments
- 2026-10-14 (review of the first full build): the QR's dark modules were voids (the whole panel was cut from the
  body); the hole's chamfer ate its 0.6 mm raised ring (now 1.0); the URL's Arial Rounded strokes and inter-glyph
  brown were under a nozzle width (now Avenir Next Bold with tracking); two glints per eye 0.15 mm apart (now one).
- 2026-10-14 (user review of the render): nose/mouth/whiskers were a tangle (whiskers dropped, spurs trimmed);
  letters G, R, &, A, P were lumpy (smoothed); the ribbon's folds no longer reached the ring after the drop (swept
  down to meet it); the ring's paw prints sat at uneven radii (stamped evenly on one circle).
- 2026-10-14: started as a face-down flat inlay (≈15 swaps); the user chose a face-up relief (cat above the disc,
  paws and eyes above the cat) with the back on the plate instead. Stack redesigned as above.
- 2026-10-14: layers 0.16 -> 0.2 at the user's suggestion: the visible surfaces are flat tops and steps, so the
  finer layers added nothing and cost five more swaps. The stack is 16 layers, 3.2 mm.
- 2026-10-14: Bambu Studio's CLI refused the plate with "gcode unprintable, error 4": cadkit's settings template
  mixes PETG and PLA profiles and the slicer won't print both in one object. The build now copies one filament
  profile (`filament_type`, PLA) into all four slots and sets the slot colors. Sliced result: bands exactly as
  the table (white+brown 0-2, brown 3-8, orange+white 9-11, all four 12-13, white+brown 14-15), 13.7 g, ~54 min
  plus filament changes.
- 2026-10-14: back was to be a three-line yellow URL; the user asked to keep the QR. On the plate the QR costs
  the same four swaps (one inlay color), so the back is a white QR panel plus the URL in small white text.
