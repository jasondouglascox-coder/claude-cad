# Speaker shelf: design

- One black PLA piece, printed flat, no supports, no hardware (the user chose snap-only).
- The speaker sits centered between two rails (the rails are its side walls, about 1.5 mm clear each side), its
  center about 30 mm forward of the rail line, so its back still sits between the rails.
- Holding up: snap friction alone on painted metal is weak, so the offset load cocks each clip and it pinches its rail
  top-back and bottom-front (it holds when offset > clip height / (2 × friction)). The heavier the speaker, the
  harder the grip.
- Each clip: a front jaw on the tray, and a long flexing arm along the rail's outer face with a hook behind it; you push
  the tray onto the rails from the front, the hooks snap behind.
- Fit coupon first: a rail fork (slots 12.4–13.8) and a stepped gap gauge (84–88) to replace ruler numbers.

## Amendments

- 2026-11-07, after the fit coupon: clip jaw-to-hook spacing 12.3 (the 12.4 slot fit; the user asked tighter), rail gap
  88. With the wider gap the rails no longer act as the speaker's side walls: the pocket gets four walls of its own
  (1.6 mm, 0.4 mm clear of the rails). The arm sits 0.1 mm off the rail's outer face and flexes out if the gap is
  over 88.
