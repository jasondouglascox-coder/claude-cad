# Halloween gnome

A 2" (50.8 mm) witch-hat gnome that sits on top of a mini Christmas tree through a 1/2" hole in its base.

## Decisions (2026-10-29)

- **Three prints, pressed together:** the body, the hat and the nose. Colouring the nose in place would cost
  ~80 swaps (it shares layers with the beard); the user chose a separate nose that snaps on tight.
- **Colours by height, 2 swaps per multi-colour print** (over the default budget of 1, accepted):
  - body: black boots to 2.4 mm, orange robe to 11 mm, white beard and hair to the shoulder (29 mm) and the head cone;
  - hat (printed brim down): black brim 1.2 mm, orange band 2.6 mm, black crown with a crooked tip leaning back;
  - nose: pink.
- Purple was the first choice for the robe; the user has no purple spool yet, so the robe is orange (2026-10-29).
- AMS: 1 orange, 2 white, 3 black, 4 pink (the sunflower's yellow and brown swapped out).
- **Tree hole:** Ø12.7 mm, 18 mm deep, with a 40-degree pointed roof (no bridge).
- **Fits, no clearance (the user's call: a good squeeze):**
  - the nose's teardrop hole on a teardrop peg out of a flat cut in the face (point down, so the peg prints lying
    down without support; the hole is 0.2 mm deeper than the peg so the nose's flat back seats);
  - the hat's conical socket over the white head cone (35 degrees from vertical), the brim resting on the shoulder.
- **No supports anywhere:** every body is a stack of horizontal sections, none wider than the one below by more
  than 0.95 mm per mm up; the beard's strands fade in over its 45-degree underside so they don't break that.

## Amendments

- **2026-10-29, after the pre-print review:**
  - A 35-degree cone can't hold by friction (tan 35° = 0.70, against about 0.3 for PLA on PLA), so the hat would
    sit loose. The head and the socket now share a 3 mm straight collar at Ø18, a press fit, and taper above it.
  - Every hole's mouth on the bed (the tree hole, the hat socket, the nose hole) flares 0.3 mm, closing to size
    0.4 mm up, so first-layer squish never makes the mouth the tightest point of a zero-clearance fit.
  - Assembly order: the nose first, then the hat (the brim reaches out over the nose).
