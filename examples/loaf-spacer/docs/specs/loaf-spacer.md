# Loaf pan spacer: spec

A removable divider for a loaf pan, only for rising: pulled out before the pan goes in the oven.

## Shape

- The pan's inside cross-section (measured by the user): floor 65 mm wide, straight walls to 20 mm, sloping out
  to 94 mm wide at 35 mm, straight up to the rim at 60 mm. 20 → 35 mm is 15 mm of rise for 14.5 mm out per side,
  so the slope is 44 degrees; the given widths win over the "45 degrees".
- 0.3 mm clearance (`fit`) to each wall so it drops in without catching.
- Full width carries on 5 mm above the rim, then a centered 40 × 30 mm pull tab with a 22 mm finger hole.
  3 mm rounds on the sheet's top corners and the tab's roots, 8 mm on the tab's top corners.

## Print

- Lies flat: one 0.6 mm sheet (3 layers at 0.2 mm).
- Black PLA (AMS slot 3; the project names the AMS set as loaded so it opens black), one plate, no supports, no
  color changes.

## Amendments

- 2026-11-15: 0.8 mm (4 layers) → 0.6 mm (3 layers), the user's call. 2 layers was weighed and turned down:
  too floppy to set in and pull out of dough, and it curls coming off the bed.
